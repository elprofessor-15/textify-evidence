# Research evidence

Historical publication snapshots. Check source scope before reuse.

## AI adoption across organizations

2023: 55%; 2024: 72%

Metric: Respondents reporting organizational AI use
Scope: Global survey respondents

Caveat: Survey-reported adoption in at least one business function. These are separate survey waves, not a longitudinal panel. Historical snapshot, not current adoption.

Source: McKinsey & Company. The state of AI in early 2024 (2024-05-30). Exhibit 1. https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2024

## Regular generative AI use

2023: 33%; 2024: 65%

Metric: Respondents reporting regular organizational gen AI use
Scope: Global survey respondents

Caveat: Regular use in at least one business function. Survey waves differ; adoption does not establish productivity or revenue impact. Historical 2024 snapshot.

Source: McKinsey & Company. The state of AI in early 2024 (2024-05-30). Gen AI adoption findings and Exhibit 3. https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2024
